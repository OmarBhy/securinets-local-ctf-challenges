As treasurer of Securinets FST, I’ve built connections across more universes than I can count. Recently, I managed to negotiate a rare agreement with a parallel chapter: Securinets FST – Time Reverse Universe Division.

In their universe, the rules of morality—and even basic arithmetic—don’t behave the way we expect. What we call “bad behavior” here can be transformed into something valuable there.

To make this partnership work, we launched an official portal system:

    In our universe, every member receives a limited amount of HR points.
    Members can spend those points to confess infractions committed on Earth through the portal interface.
    Each confession is exchanged at a classified rate and contributes toward earning a Portal Access Key.
    With the key, a member can access the Time Reverse Universe and convert “bad behavior” into benefits under their inverted laws.

Your mission is to interact with the portal access system, understand how it evaluates confessions, and obtain the Portal Access Key to retrieve the flag.
