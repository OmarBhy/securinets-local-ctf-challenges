# Mirror Universe

**Category:** Reverse Engineering  
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard  
**Points:** 400

---

## Description

A breach from a parallel dimension has locked the SecuriNets FST system. 
To restore access, you must find a recovery key that satisfies two 
"dimensional laws" - mysterious transformations that protect the system.

The first law deals with reflection and shifting letters through the mirror. 
The second law manipulates reality at the bit level, with behavior that 
changes based on position.

⚠️ **Warning:** The keys are derived at runtime - static analysis alone won't 
reveal everything. You'll need to observe the system's execution.

---

## Details

- **Flag format:** `Securinets_fst{...}`
- **Binary:** ELF 64-bit, stripped, no PIE
- **Recommended tools:** Ghidra + GDB/Pwndbg

---

## Hints

- Two parts, two different transformations
- Keys are calculated dynamically
- Some classic cryptographic operations are involved
- The system tells you which part is wrong - use that feedback!
